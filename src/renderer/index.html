Photos Backup

Desktop backup to Google Photos

Back up your photos to Google Photos

Connect a Google account, choose the folders to back up, and the app uploads each photo and video in original quality (or Storage Saver). Items already in Google Photos are skipped automatically.

  1. Sign in with Google in a private window — the token never leaves this machine.
  2. Pick the folders that hold your library.
  3. Press Back up now. That's it.

Uses Google's private Photos endpoints; the integration may stop working without notice. Experimental software — use at your own risk.
